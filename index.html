SUBMISSION / ST-1048
IN REVIEW

This study evaluates the impact of automated scoring models on undergraduate research projects. Using a sample size of N=142 across four academic terms, we analyzed score distributions and rubric consistency before and after implementing structured evaluation criteria.

Our quantitative analysis indicates a statistically significant improvement in scoring reliability (p < .01), with rubric alignment increasing by 14.2% across peer-reviewed evaluations. Furthermore, qualitative analysis of student feedback demonstrated higher overall clarity and timeliness during evaluation cycles.
